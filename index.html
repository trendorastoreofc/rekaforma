Both faces of the flower move on a keyboard switch. Press anywhere on the front and the green band closes 4 mm, then springs back. Something to do with your hands in a meeting.

Inside

Five printed parts and one switch.

  1. Front centre

    Glued into its own pocket. A separate print so the colour edge stays crisp.

    ~1.5 g

  2. Front petals

    One colour, printed flower side up, no supports.

    ~7 g

  3. Band

    A thin ring that follows the petal outline and hides the switch.

    ~2 g

  4. MX switch

    A standard mechanical keyboard switch, clipped in.

    1 pc

  5. Back petals and centre

    The same flower on the other side, so it looks right whichever way it lands.

    ~9.5 g

The studio

Reka + forma.

rekaMalay. To design, to invent, to make up.
formaForm. The shape a thing takes in your hand.

Rekaforma is a small design studio in Kuala Lumpur. We do not print other people's files. Every object here starts as our own sketch, goes through rounds of prototypes on our own printers, and only reaches this shop once it feels right to hold.

We start with things that are familiar: a flower, a kuih, a kampung window. Then we make them into something you can use every day.
