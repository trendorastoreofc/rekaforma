How it works

A keyboard switch, set inside a flower.

Five printed parts stack around one mechanical switch. Press the petals and the whole face travels 4 mm, closes the green band with a click, and springs back. Every colour is its own part, so there is no paint to wear off.

How we work

Our own designs only

Printed in-house

Made in small batches

We print on our own machines in Kuala Lumpur, a few at a time, and check each piece by hand before it ships.

Built from parts
