Why flowers

A flower says
I thought of you.

People have given each other flowers for thousands of years, and almost never because they were useful. A flower is a way of saying: I noticed you. I thought of you when you were not here.

Our first series began with someone we love, who has always loved flowers. We wanted to make one that would outlast the week, and that asks to be held, not only admired.

Small, repeated movements are one of the oldest ways people settle a busy mind. A thumb on a smooth stone. A pen that clicks. A bead turned between the fingers. Put that habit inside a flower and it changes meaning. The click becomes a small moment of care, for yourself or for the person who gave it to you.

Real flowers bloom once, and that is part of why we remember them. Ours do too.

How it works

A keyboard switch, set inside a flower.

Five printed parts stack around one mechanical switch. Press the petals and the whole face travels 4 mm, closes the band between the two faces with a click, and springs back. Every colour is its own part, so there is no paint to wear off.

How we work

Designed here

Our own designs only

We do not print other people's files. Every object starts as a sketch in our studio and is modelled from scratch.

Blooms once

Made once, with care

We print in small batches so we can look after every piece and check its quality by hand. When a batch sells out, we do not reprint it. We move on to the next series.

Built from parts

Colour that lasts

Each colour is printed as its own part and fitted together, instead of painted on, so the finish does not chip or fade.
