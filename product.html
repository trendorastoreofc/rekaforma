What's in the box

The Flower Clicker and a key ring, ready to clip onto your keys or bag.

Materials and construction

Five parts printed in PLA, one colour each: front petals, back petals, two centres and a thin band. The centres are glued into their own pockets, so every colour edge is crisp and nothing is painted.

Inside is an MX-style clicky blue switch, the loudest and most tactile kind of keyboard switch: you feel a bump and hear a sharp click on every press.

Care

PLA softens in heat. Keep it out of a parked car in the afternoon sun. Wipe clean with a damp cloth.
