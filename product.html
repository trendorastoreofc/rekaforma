What is inside

Five printed parts: front petals, back petals, two centres and a thin band. The centres are separate prints glued into their pockets, so the colour edge is clean instead of painted.

The switch is a standard mechanical keyboard switch. Press the front and the whole face moves down 4 mm, closing the band.

Care

PLA softens in heat. Keep it out of a car dashboard in the afternoon sun. Wipe with a damp cloth.
