Questions

Does it work as a keyboard key?

No. It uses a real keyboard switch for the feel and the sound, but it is not wired to anything. It is a fidget object, not a gadget.

Is it loud?

Yes, it is meant to be. It uses a clicky blue switch, the loudest and most tactile kind of keyboard switch. You will hear a sharp click across a quiet room, so it is better at home or at your desk than in a library.

What is it made of?

PLA, a plant-based plastic, printed in five parts with one colour each. There is no paint, so the colours will not chip.

What comes in the box?

The Flower Clicker and a key ring.

Can I choose my own colours?

Not yet. Each flower comes in the colours shown in the shop.
